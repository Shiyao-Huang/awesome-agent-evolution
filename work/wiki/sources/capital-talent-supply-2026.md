# Source: Capital, Talent Supply & Stakeholder Perspectives 2026

> **Type**: Source analysis | **Rank**: A | **Date**: 2026-05-26

## Key Findings

1. **Talent pipeline crisis**: US international AI researcher inflow dropped 89% (Stanford 2026); 36% AI jobs unfilled; entry-level dev employment -20%
2. **Capital misalignment**: AI VC $200B+ but self-evolution receives <1%; Recursive Superintelligence ($650M) is sole major exception
3. **Academic→Industry drain**: CEPR tracks 42K researchers — top 1% industry earn $1.5M more/year than academics (5x increase since 2001); papers decrease, patents increase
4. **China catching up**: Lemon AI (L3 self-evolving Agent, 千万天使轮), 中数睿智 (数亿), leveraging DeepSeek/Qwen open-source
5. **Recursive thesis**: "50,000 PhD-level system" as first goal; 4 employees are DGM co-authors; GV invests in team, not product

## Capital Flow Network

```
$200B+ AI total → chips/infra 40% → model training 30% → apps/agent 20% → self-evolution <1%
Self-evolution: Recursive $650M + Sakana $365M = almost all direct capital
```
