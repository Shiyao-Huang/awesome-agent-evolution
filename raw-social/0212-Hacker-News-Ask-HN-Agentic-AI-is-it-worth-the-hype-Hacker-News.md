Ask HN: Agentic AI, is it worth the hype? | Hacker News Agentic AI, is it worth the hype? What is working and what is not? That depends if you're asking as an investor, or asking as someone who wants If you want to use the tools, and you don't mind that they're behaving like someone who has only recently graduated and therefore still needs a lot of handholding (i.e. not actually "agentic") for anything above junior-grade, they are absolutely worth it, being much cheaper than any human anywhere in the world. But the limit is they act like juniors, and will say "yes certainly" in a naïve-looking or fawning fashion to social-engineering-pattern attacks. You can replace juniors, but only juniors.
