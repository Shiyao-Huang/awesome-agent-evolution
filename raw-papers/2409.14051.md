# GroupDebate: Enhancing the Efficiency of Multi-Agent Debate Using Group Discussion

- **arXiv ID**: 2409.14051
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.14051
- **PDF**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.14051
- **Authors**: Tongxuan Liu, Xingyu Wang, Weizhe Huang, Wenjiang Xu, Yuting Zeng, Lei Jiang, Hailong Yang, Jing Li
- **Year**: 2024

## Abstract

Method to significantly reduce token cost in multi-agent debates by dividing agents into multiple debate groups, with agents debating within groups and sharing interim results between groups. Reduces total tokens by up to 51.7% while potentially enhancing accuracy by up to 25%.
