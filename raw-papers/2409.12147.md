# MAgICoRe: Multi-Agent, Iterative, Coarse-to-Fine Refinement for Reasoning

- **arXiv ID**: 2409.12147
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12147
- **PDF**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.12147
- **Authors**: Justin Chih-Yao Chen et al.
- **Year**: 2024

## Abstract

Multi-agent framework that categorizes problem difficulty, solving easy problems with coarse-grained aggregation and hard ones with fine-grained iterative multi-agent refinement. Three agents: Solver, Reviewer (generates targeted feedback based on step-wise RM scores), and Refiner (incorporates feedback). One iteration beats Self-Consistency by 3.4%, Best-of-k by 3.2%, and Self-Refine by 4.0%.
