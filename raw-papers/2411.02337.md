## Abstract

Self-evolving online curriculum RL framework for training high-performance web agents using open LLMs. Incorporates self-evolving curriculum generating new tasks from unsuccessful attempts, outcome-supervised reward model, and adaptive RL strategies. Improves Llama-3.1-8B from 4.8% to 42.4% on WebArena-Lite, surpassing GPT-4-Turbo (17.6%).
