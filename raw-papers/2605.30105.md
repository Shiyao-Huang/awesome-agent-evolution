# Learning from Repair Experience: Self-Evolving Agents for Automated Vulnerability Repair

**Authors**: Haichuan Hu, Guoqing Xie, Quanjun Zhang, Jiawei Liu, Shengcheng Yu, Chunrong Fang, Zhenyu Chen, Liang Xiao

**Submitted**: 2026-05-28

**Venue**: ICSE 2027 (accepted)

**Code**: https://github.com/iSEngLab/Learn-from-Repair-Experience

## Abstract

Large Language Models (LLMs) have shown promise for automated vulnerability repair (AVR), but they still face several limitations, including the lack of intra-vulnerability experience accumulation and the lack of cross-vulnerability experience reuse. As a result, LLMs may repeatedly make similar mistakes during iterative repair and underutilize valuable repair knowledge from historical vulnerabilities. To address these challenges, we propose EvoRepair, an experience-based self-evolving AVR agent framework that enables LLMs to accumulate, refine, and leverage domain-specific knowledge across long-horizon vulnerability repairs. EvoRepair follows a cyclic learn-and-repair process that retrieves relevant past experiences to guide repair, extracts new experiences from repair trajectories, and updates an experience bank using quality-aware scoring. We evaluate EvoRepair against 15 representative vulnerability repair baselines on PATCHEVAL and SEC-bench. Results show that EvoRepair achieves the best overall performance, reaching 93.47% on PATCHEVAL, 87.00% on SEC-bench, and 90.46% overall. In particular, EvoRepair outperforms the latest LLM-based baseline LoopRepair by 39.56% and 33.50% on PATCHEVAL and SEC-bench, respectively, and surpasses IntentFix by 70.86% and 50.50%. Across both benchmarks, EvoRepair also exceeds the recent self-evolving agent Live-SWE-Agent by 6.98% overall. Additional transfer experiments on VUL4J further demonstrate the robustness of EvoRepair across models, programming languages, and datasets. These findings demonstrate that experience-based self-evolution substantially strengthens agentic AVR and goes beyond existing self-evolving techniques.

## Key Contributions

1. **EvoRepair experience-bank AVR agent**: A cyclic learn-and-repair loop that retrieves, extracts, and quality-scores repair experiences so an AVR agent improves across long-horizon vulnerability repairs without weight updates.
2. **Quality-aware experience bank updates**: New trajectories are scored and written into a persistent bank that supports intra- and cross-vulnerability reuse.
3. **Strong empirical results with transfer**: Best overall results on PatchEval and SEC-bench versus 15 baselines, with additional Vul4J transfer across models, languages, and datasets.

## Relevance to Agent Evolution

What evolves: the external experience bank (repair knowledge), not model weights. Feedback signal: repair success and quality-aware scores derived from repair trajectories. Verification: PatchEval, SEC-bench, and Vul4J transfer. This is an Observe–Modify–Verify loop over persistent experiential memory for coding/AVR agents.
