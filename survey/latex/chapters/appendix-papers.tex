\subsection*{A.6 Key Observations}

\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item \textbf{Architecture evolution dominates.} Over half of the surveyed projects focus on evolving agent or workflow structure rather than just prompts or parameters. This reflects the field's shift from prompt engineering toward structural self-modification.

\item \textbf{Stars correlate with developer tooling, not research depth.} The highest-starred projects (AutoGPT 175K, Cline 58K, OpenHands 55K) are end-user developer tools, not research frameworks. Research-only systems like ADAS and DGM have no separate GitHub stars.

\item \textbf{MIT and Apache 2.0 dominate licensing.} Only AutoML-Agent uses CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial). DEAP uses LGPLv3. This permissive licensing landscape facilitates cross-project adoption and integration.

\item \textbf{Evolution modes are converging.} The most recent frameworks (EvoAgentX, LLaMEA, OpenTreeSearch) combine multiple evolution modes---prompt optimization, architecture search, and reward-driven feedback---into unified pipelines.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Researcher Lineage and Collaboration Network}
\label{app:lineage}

This appendix maps the intellectual heritage and collaboration structure of the agent self-evolution field. We identify three foundational lineages that converge to produce the current research landscape, along with key institutional clusters and advisor-advisee relationships.

\subsection*{D.1 Three Foundational Lineages}

The field of agent self-evolution draws from three distinct intellectual traditions that have converged over the 2022--2026 period:

\textbf{Lineage 1: Evolutionary Computation.} Miikkulainen $\to$ Stanley $\to$ Lehman $\to$ Clune $\to$ Hu/Lu/Zhang. This lineage traces from classical neuroevolution (NEAT, 2002) through open-ended evolution (Novelty Search, POET) to automated agent architecture design (ADAS, DGM). The lineage is characterized by its emphasis on population-level search, quality-diversity optimization, and open-endedness.

\textbf{Lineage 2: Language Agent Reasoning.} Narasimhan $\to$ Yao/Shinn/Jimenez. This lineage traces from foundational language model work (GPT-1) through reasoning frameworks (ReAct, Reflexion, Tree of Thought) to autonomous software engineering (SWE-Bench, SWE-Agent). The lineage is characterized by its emphasis on natural language as the medium for agent reasoning and self-correction.

\textbf{Lineage 3: LLM Self-Improvement.} Madaan/Clark/Welleck. This lineage traces from iterative self-refinement (Self-Refine) through multi-turn introspection (RISE) to broader self-improvement paradigms. The lineage bridges CMU, AI2, and Google, and is characterized by its emphasis on prompt-time and weight-level self-improvement without external architectural changes.
