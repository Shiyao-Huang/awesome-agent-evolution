After a week of researching different AI agent frameworks, I’ve put together insights that might help other builders:Ecosystem: LangGraph, CrewAI, and Autogen (plus the widely discussed LangChain) are the most commented right now. Semantic Kernel is there too, but it’s more niche.Autogen Warning: Autogen is popular but not production-ready. It’s undergoing rapid changes and may merge into Semantic Kernel, forcing future migration work.Ease of Use vs Customization: CrewAI is the simplest to get started with. LangGraph gives you more freedom if you need complexity and flexibility.Framework Popularity vs. Utility: Popular frameworks aren’t always the most stable or developer-friendly. Don’t base your decision only in Github starsSemantic Kernel: More oriented towards C#/.NET community. Not very appealing for Python devsTakeaway: If you’re just starting, go simple. Try CrewAI or even a no-code tool like n8n. Only switch to something more robust (like LangGraph) when you hit the limits.What are your experiences with AI Agent frameworks?nachopujol on Dec 18, 2024  | prev [–]
